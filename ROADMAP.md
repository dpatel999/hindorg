# Hindustan Organics – Strategic Roadmap (2026–2030)

## Current Reality
- Manufacturer of high-purity industrial solvents & specialty chemicals (Ahmedabad)
- Strong domestic customer base (500+ clients)
- New focus: North American expansion via X9Elysium (Canada + USA)
- Professional corporate website live at hindorg.com
- Long-term ambition: Prepare the company for public listing

## 2026 – Foundation & Professionalization
- Strengthen financial reporting, governance, and middle management
- Execute X9Elysium aggressively (build real revenue in Canada/USA)
- Improve brand visibility and positioning
- Clean up operations and documentation

## 2027–2028 – Scale & Proof
- X9Elysium becomes a meaningful growth engine
- Expand high-value product lines (pharma-grade, low-moisture, custom blends)
- Build consistent, audited growth track record
- Start selective investor conversations

## 2028–2030 – Pre-IPO to Listing
- Full corporate governance (independent directors, proper board, ESOPs)
- Engage bankers and prepare for listing
- Target realistic IPO window once revenue, margins, and governance are strong enough

**Note:** This is a serious, multi-year journey. The website and X9Elysium narrative are important, but the real work is in numbers, governance, and execution.
